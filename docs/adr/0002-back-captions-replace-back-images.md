# Back captions replace images of a print's back

When the back of a print has writing, Yomi stores the recognized text as the photo's caption and does not save an image of the back. Apple Photos has no way to pair a back with its photo, and a caption is searchable and travels with the photo. The cost is that writing Yomi cannot read, or reads wrongly, is lost unless the print is scanned again with a document scan profile.
